# Holiday mode

Triggered when the user's message contains a trigger word (any language, case-insensitive). The holiday adds elements to the agent's own story; it never replaces the story or the brand palette.

| Holiday | Triggers | Props | Settings | Accent colors (small doses) | Music elements | Copy angle | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Christmas | christmas, xmas, 圣诞, holiday season | knit scarf, tiny Santa hat, fairy lights, gift box, pine sprig | snowy window, cozy living room | deep red, pine green, warm gold | sleigh bells, celesta | warm sharing, cozy | religious imagery unless the brand uses it |
| Lunar New Year | lunar new year, chinese new year, cny, spring festival, 春节, 新年, 过年 | red envelope, lantern, couplet shapes (no text), firecracker confetti, zodiac animal | family table, lantern-lit street | festive red, gold | guzheng, small gong, lively pipa | reunion, luck | readable characters (overlay real text in post instead) |
| Mid-Autumn | mid-autumn, moon festival, 中秋 | mooncake, full moon, rabbit, lantern | rooftop under the moon | moon gold, night indigo | erhu, soft flute | togetherness, longing | — |
| Valentine's | valentine, 情人节, 520, qixi, 七夕 | heart-shaped confetti, two straws, love letter (no text) | park bench, café table | blush pink, soft red | soft guitar, glockenspiel | sharing a cup | suggestive content |
| Halloween | halloween, 万圣节 | pumpkin, cute ghost, bat, witch hat | moonlit street, candlelight | pumpkin orange, purple | pizzicato, playful spooky bells | cute-spooky | gore, real scares |
| Thanksgiving | thanksgiving, 感恩节 | autumn leaves, pie, cozy blanket | autumn park, dinner table | maple orange, brown | acoustic folk | gratitude | — |
| Easter | easter, 复活节 | pastel eggs, bunny ears | spring garden | pastel yellow, mint | light woodwinds | spring, fresh | religious imagery unless the brand uses it |
| Diwali | diwali, deepavali, 排灯节 | diyas (small lamps), rangoli patterns, marigolds | doorway with lamps | marigold, warm gold | sitar shimmer, tabla-lite | light, joy | fire hazards, religious icons |
| Eid / Ramadan | eid, ramadan, 开斋节, 斋月 | crescent moon, lanterns, dates | evening sky after sunset | emerald, gold | oud, soft percussion | sharing after sunset | **no alcohol cues**, no daytime eating or drinking during Ramadan scenes, religious figures |
| Songkran | songkran, 泼水节 | water splashes, flower garlands | sunny street | aqua, flower colors | light Thai-pop percussion | fun, refresh | — |
| Summer | summer, 夏天, 夏日, heatwave | sun, sunglasses, ice cubes, fan | beach, hot street | sky blue, sun yellow | ukulele, steel drum | cool down | — |

If a holiday isn't listed, design its elements the same way (props, setting, accent colors, music, copy angle, sensitivities), and add a note to the report.
